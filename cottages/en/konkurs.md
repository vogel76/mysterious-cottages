# Tymelion and Matylda

> Not long ago, when the morning dew still could not decide whether it was more water or more glitter, the cottage of a remarkable pair of siblings appeared among the rocks.
> In it lived Tymelion and his sister, Matylda the Giggler.
> Tymelion was convinced that the world had been created so that he could cross it. By dawn he was already running along the forest paths. Whenever he came upon water — he jumped in without unnecessary questions. A lake, a river, a pond… and once, reportedly, an enormous puddle, which he proudly named the "micro-ocean". The season of the year never had any say in it. In winter he said the water toughens you up. In summer, that it cools you down. In autumn, that it is romantic. In spring, that there is no time to waste on excuses.
> Matylda was his complete opposite. By day she laughed so often that the woodpeckers would sometimes stop their tapping out of pure curiosity. And when dusk fell, she would sit in front of the cottage and hold long conversations with the Moon. Nobody ever heard the answers, but from the look on her face you could tell that the Moon has a really good sense of humour.
> One pair of siblings. Two different worlds.
> He collected places on the map and new friends.
> She collected stars that nobody but her could find.
> And yet something bound them together more strongly than family ties.
> They both believed that the greatest treasures are found by those who do not sit still.
> Tymelion set out on the road for new adventures.
> Matylda set out in her thoughts to places others never even looked at.
> And that is probably why their cottage appeared right here. To remind everyone that a person needs two things: the courage to explore the world… and the imagination to truly see it.
> The moral?
> It does not matter whether you conquer mountain peaks or count stars. What matters most is to discover, every single day, something that makes you come home with a smile.
