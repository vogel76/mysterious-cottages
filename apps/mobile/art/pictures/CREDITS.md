# The room's paintings

`painting.jpg` is a photograph of a real painting by the artist who builds
and tends the Chatynkowo cottages in the field and whose idea the app is.
The owner supplied the photograph on 2026-11-20 for the dearest piece of
the elf's room (`picture_painting`: `build_picture_painting` in
`art/blender/room.py` puts it on the canvas of a gilded frame). The file
is the photograph scaled to 1024 pixels across, nothing else done to it.

All rights to the painting stay with its author.
