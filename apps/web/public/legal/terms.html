Chatynkowo — Terms of Service

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. About the Service

Chatynkowo (the “Service”) is the fairytale website www.chatynkowo.pl and the Chatynkowo mobile app for Android and iOS, inspired by the chatynkowo.pl project. The Service shows a map and descriptions of hidden “Elves' Cottages” and lets you unlock their stories by entering or scanning the code found on the plaque next to each cottage.

The app is also home to an elf you can look after: feed it, play with it, play games and furnish its cottage.

The Service is operated by BlockchainWares Software, entered in the Polish National Court Register (KRS) under number 0000137120 (the “Operator”).

Using the Service is free of charge and voluntary. All cottages, stories and rewards are available at no cost and without an account. Looking after the elf and the games are free as well. Only the voluntary purchases described in sections 4 and 5 are paid.

2. Account and leaderboard

An account is optional. You create it by signing in with Google or Apple. It keeps your finds, and your elf with its acorns and star dust, when you switch devices and lets you take part in the public seekers' leaderboard, which shows your nickname, your account photo (if you turn it on) and your progress in finding the cottages. The separate Supporters' Leaderboard (section 4) lists only accounts that agreed to be on it.

  • Your nickname must not break the law or infringe anyone's rights, impersonate other people, or be offensive or vulgar. The Operator may reset such a nickname to the default one.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app (the Profile tab, the Account screen, the Delete account button) or at www.chatynkowo.pl/delete-account.html.

How we process data is described in the Privacy Policy.

4. Voluntary support and the Supporters' Leaderboard (app)

In the app you can voluntarily support Chatynkowo by buying “a coffee for the elf” or by watching an ad. A coffee unlocks nothing in the game, and no support affects the seekers' leaderboard.

A signed-in user may join the Supporters' Leaderboard. Places on it go by points: 1 point for every ad watched to the end and 25 to 250 points for a coffee, depending on its size. A place on it brings no rewards and no advantage in the game. Taking part needs your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the account settings.

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store on their terms, including payments and refunds. The ad is served by Google AdMob only when you ask for it.

5. The elf, acorns and star dust (app)

Acorns and star dust are virtual items in the game with the elf. Acorns are earned in the game only. Star dust is earned in the game, for watching an ad (up to a daily limit), or bought in packs through Google Play or the App Store. The price of a pack in real money is shown before the purchase.

Dust can shorten a wait (for the egg to hatch, for example) and buy the star brew, one more life in a game, and furniture and hats for the elf. An ad can also give the elf energy or one more life in a game, or double the day's reward. Everything the game makes you wait for also comes by itself, without paying and without ads.

  • Acorns and dust have no monetary value. They cannot be exchanged for money or passed to another person.
  • The elf's state, the acorns and the dust, bought dust included, are kept on your device and, for a signed-in user, on their account as well, from which they return after a reinstall or on a new phone. An account keeps one elf. If the device and the account hold two different elves, the more experienced one stays and takes the other's star dust.
  • Without an account the elf, the acorns and the dust are kept on the device only. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data or switching phones then removes them for good, and the Operator cannot restore them.
  • “Start the elf over” removes the elf from the device and from the account.
  • Bought dust reaches the game as soon as the store confirms the payment. Refunds and payment complaints are handled by the store the purchase was made in, on its terms.
  • The elf's reminders are optional and can be turned off in the app at any time.
