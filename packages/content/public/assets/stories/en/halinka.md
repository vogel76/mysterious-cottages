Quite recently…

…magic decided to give Halinka the elf a little surprise.

With a single gust, it carried her cottage onto the spreading bough of an old tree, right beside the rocks about which people had whispered for generations that witches used to meet there.

Halinka looked around, cursed in astonishment, and smiled.

"A beautiful spot. Only now everyone will take me for a witch. And I'm just an ordinary elf, after all."

Because Halinka didn't know a single spell.

For nearly three hundred years she had been busy with something far more important — she found human dreams that had been abandoned too soon. She cleaned the dust of doubt off them, darned them with spider's thread, and set them on a forest shelf until their owner dared to reach for them again.

So if one day, passing that little cottage, you suddenly remember an old dream of your own… don't go looking for witches.

That's just Halinka, back at work.

Moral:

The greatest magic usually doesn't throw off dazzling sparks or burst into glitter — it simply works quietly, the moment someone finally stops saying "it's too late now" and starts saying "oh, what the heck, I'll give it a try."

Try to remember yours!!!!
