# Boguś's Cottage

> **Not so long ago…**
> 
> …there lived an Elf called Boguś. Just an ordinary Boguś. Well, sometimes a Bogumił is born too, but this was not one of those cases.
> With his own hands Boguś shaped a cottage out of clay and stones right in the middle of the Łabajowa Cave. Any normal Elf would say it is damp, dark and smelly, but Boguś decided that at least the neighbours never drill ;)
> Boguś is constantly on the move. There is not a single moment when he is doing nothing. Well… unless he is asleep. Then even the bats walk on tiptoe.
> Every day he bustles about the cave and cleans up after people. Because people have a remarkable gift. They can marvel at a million-year-old rock, take fifty photos with it, and then leave behind such a mess as if they wanted to add a geological layer of their own.
> Boguś grumbles under his breath, calls them a few names, but then he sweeps up and goes back to his own affairs. And he has plenty of those. He even helped with the filming of that music video by Dawid, the one who "wins people over". Nobody noticed him there, but apparently the stones for the campfire arranged themselves. A coincidence? Elves do not believe in coincidences.
> Do you know why Boguś settled right here?
> Because Elves know something that people often remember only when it is already too late!
> 
> **Boguś claims that as long as you have a reason to get out of bed in the morning, your birth certificate can safely stay in the drawer. And even the Elves do not argue with that.**
> 
> <br>
>     **The moral? The ones most needed are often those whom someone was too quick to call unneeded.**
