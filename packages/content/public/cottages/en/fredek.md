# The Cottage of Ferdek the Elf and the Bat

> Ferdek spent a long time looking for a place where he would feel truly elf-like. He walked among the rocks, peered under roots and climbed old trees, but nowhere was quite right.
> 
> <br>
> One evening he heard a thin squeak in his ears. At first it was strange and quiet, but after a moment it turned into a beautiful melody. Ferdek followed the sound, and then he spotted a small bat leading him deeper and deeper among the Jurassic rocks.
> 
> The Elf stumbled over stones and over his own feet along the way, but he stubbornly followed the melody. At last he arrived at a great cave. It is dark, cold and damp in there — so not exactly pleasant, but the bat pointed him to a little crack in the rock, with the tangled roots of a vine under a 200-year-old linden tree, right here!
> 
> Ferdek set to work at once. Out of moss, wood, a few crooked sticks and everything he could find nearby, he built a little cottage.
> The roof is a bit crooked, the windows are tiny, and the door is so low that even he has to stoop.
> 
> But that was exactly what he wanted.
> 
> In the evenings he sits in front of the cottage together with the bats and listens to their melodious squeaks. And when the wind slips into the cave, the melody drifts quietly back among the rocks, as if the place itself were singing the Elves a lullaby.
> 
> And the moral of this story is this: sometimes you have to search for a long time and trust even the strangest melody to find the place where your heart feels at home. Fredek will tell you that the forest folk hide their greatest treasures not in chests at all, but in silence. If you ever hear the mosses whispering and the ferns nodding along, know that it is Fredek rehearsing his newest song.
