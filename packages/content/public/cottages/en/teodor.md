# The Cottage of Teodor the Elf

> Teodor is a collector of falling leaves. He has a chest of them bigger than he is.

He says every leaf remembers the year in which it fell. When he lays them out in a circle, a whole calendar of the forest appears — and the wind reads it like a book.

## Who lives here

**Teodor the Elf**, who teaches wanderers the virtue of *patience*.

## What to do when you reach the cottage?

1. Pause for a moment, close your eyes and listen to the forest.
2. Find the plaque near the cottage.
3. Scan the QR code or enter the four-digit code on the main page to hear the Elf's voice.
4. Leave the cottage just as you found it — the forest remembers.
