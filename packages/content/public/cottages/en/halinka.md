# Halinka the Elf

> **Quite recently…**
> 
> <br>
> …magic decided to play a little surprise on Halinka the Elf.
> 
> With a single gust it carried her cottage onto the sprawling bough of an old tree, right next to the rocks where, as people have whispered for generations, witches used to meet.
> Halinka looked around, swore in astonishment, and smiled.
> — A beautiful spot. Only now everyone will take me for a witch. And I am just an ordinary Elf.
> Because Halinka knew no spells at all.
> For nearly three hundred years she had been busy with something far more important — finding human dreams abandoned too soon. She dusted off their doubt, darned them with spider's thread and set them on a forest shelf, until their owner dared to reach for them again.
> So if one day, near this cottage, you suddenly remember an old dream of yours… do not go looking for witches.
> It was only Halinka, at work again.
> **The moral:**
> **The greatest magic rarely makes dazzling flashes or bursts into glitter — it simply works quietly, the moment someone finally stops saying "it's too late" and starts saying "oh well, I'll give it a try".**
> **Try to remember!!!!**
