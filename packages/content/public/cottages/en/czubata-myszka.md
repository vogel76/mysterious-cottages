# The Cottage of Czubata and Myszka

> Czubata is an Elf-maid with a lush hairdo of moss, and Myszka is her best companion, with a long tail and an even longer list of ideas.

Together they run a little library of rustling leaves. Every leaf is one story; whoever comes and listens to the rustle has borrowed a book.

## Who lives here

**Czubata and Myszka**, who teach wanderers the virtue of *imagination*.

## What to do when you reach the cottage?

1. Pause for a moment, close your eyes and listen to the forest.
2. Find the plaque near the cottage.
3. Scan the QR code or enter the four-digit code on the main page to hear the Elf's voice.
4. Leave the cottage just as you found it — the forest remembers.
