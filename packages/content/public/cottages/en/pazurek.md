# Pazurek the Elf

> In a rocky gorge, where the stone walls stand so close together that an echo can come back three times, there are two little houses.
> The first is old, mossy and leaning slightly, as if someone had stepped out for berries long ago… and forgotten to come back.
> The second is wedged so cleverly between the rocks that even its red shutters don't catch the eye. You need a truly keen eye to spot it.
> But the truth is that the old house did not empty by itself.
> Over the years people pulled off its door, broke off its shutters and took its little steps apart for souvenirs or "for fun". Everyone took a piece… until there was nothing left to take.
> The elf who lived there had had enough.
> His name is Pazurek.
> He snorted, shook the dust off his hood and built a new house, deep between the rocks, in a spot so narrow that you really have to try to notice it.
> If you find it, it means Pazurek has trusted you.
> And that he hopes this time his home will stand for a very, very long time.
> Why does he live in this gorge at all?
> Because this is where small birds build their nests in spring, where hedgehogs curl up for their winter sleep and where the deer come to graze.
> Pazurek is the forest vet.
> He bandages wings, pulls thorns out of little paws, sets strained hooves and makes sure nobody falls ill.
> So if you come to this place…
> keep quiet.
> Don't shout.
> Don't knock stones together.
> Don't peek into the nests.
> Look around carefully.
> Someone in the shade of the rocks may be getting better right now.
> And somewhere between the stones Pazurek nods with approval, seeing that you know how to be a guest in the forest, not a storm!!!
