# The Cottage of Myrk the Elf

> Not so long ago, when the Black Forest was still a place people feared to enter after sunset, an Elf named Myrk appeared.
> Nobody knows where he came from.
> His cottage was not built of wood. It was built of stones, moss and the skulls of forest creatures that have long since belonged only to the forest. They say each one reminds Myrk of someone who once wandered too far.
> He lights no fire. He does not sing. He leaves no tracks.
> And yet, at times, you may get the feeling that someone has been walking in your exact footsteps for many minutes.
> People tell all kinds of stories.
> Some claim they saw his cottage for only a split second. Others swear they found it… but the next day they could not even find the path they took back. The Google Maps specialists claim it keeps changing its position in the satellite photos.
> There is one more thing.
> If you suddenly notice there are more mosquitoes than usual, do not wave them away. They are not looking for you. They are leading you to Myrk.
> They say the mosquitoes are his eyes. They land on your shoulders, circle above your head and make sure no one approaches the cottage unseen.
> There are also those who say that when you touch the stone lying in front of his house, for a moment the forest stops making any sound at all.
> And then all you can hear is the buzzing of hundreds of mosquitoes.
> If you ever come upon a place where even the wind avoids the trees, and a dark cloud of mosquitoes hangs over the moss, do not turn back.
> It means Myrk already knows you are here.
> …do not call him.
> Do not look inside.
> And under no circumstances check whether he really is standing behind you.
> Most people think it is only a legend.
> Most.
> Are you ready to find Myrk's cottage?
