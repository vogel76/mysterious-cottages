# The Green-Haired Elf's Cottage

> His hair is moss, and his beard — a fern. When he walks, lilies of the valley spring up behind him.

He is the oldest gardener of Chatynkowo. He teaches that the world does not belong to those who own it, but to those who hear it.

## Who lives here

**The Green-Haired Elf**, who teaches wanderers the virtue of *respect*.

## What to do when you reach the cottage?

1. Pause for a moment, close your eyes and listen to the forest.
2. Find the plaque near the cottage.
3. Scan the QR code or enter the four-digit code on the main page to hear the Elf's voice.
4. Leave the cottage just as you found it — the forest remembers.
