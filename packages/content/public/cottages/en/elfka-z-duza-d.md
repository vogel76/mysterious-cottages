# The Cottage of the Elf-maid with a big b...

> Some say the "b" stands for benevolence, others — for a big soul. The truth is that the Elf-maid simply has a very big heart.

Under her cottage there is always a bowl of milk for the hedgehog and a handful of acorns for the squirrel. And when someone gets lost, the Elf-maid walks out barefoot onto the clearing and calls them by name — even if she has never met them.

## Who lives here

**The Elf-maid with a big b...**, who teaches wanderers the virtue of *kindness*.

## What to do when you reach the cottage?

1. Pause for a moment, close your eyes and listen to the forest.
2. Find the plaque near the cottage.
3. Scan the QR code or enter the four-digit code on the main page to hear the Elf's voice.
4. Leave the cottage just as you found it — the forest remembers.
