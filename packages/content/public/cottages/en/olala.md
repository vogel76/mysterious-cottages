# Ola and Zojka's Cottage

> **Ola and Zojka**
> Ola and Zojka met… in the bathroom. They lived one above the other, like Paweł and Gaweł, though they got along far better. Before Ola moved into her cottage in the forest, she lived in an old grey block of flats where you could talk to the neighbours through the pipes. And that is how she often talked to Zojka and her daughter Basia. She was Basia's favourite auntie, and the favourite of many other little sprites too.
> Ola was an artist. Not the kind that seeks publicity, fame and money. She was the kind who could find inspiration where others saw rubbish. An old window turned into a painting, scraps of fabric, newspapers and papers into landscapes, and twigs and pebbles closed in a frame into a cat sitting on a fence.
> She loved the forest and knew it like few others. She knew where the chanterelles, the birch boletes, the wild strawberries and the most beautiful ferns grew. When Basia grew up and came with a little sprite of her own, Ola showed him all the forest's secrets. Well, almost all. Elves never give everything away, after all.
> Ola and Zojka shared one more weakness — cats. The animals knew perfectly well which elves to come to when they needed help. Word of their big hearts spread quickly through the forest, and thanks to them more than one kitten found a home.
> In the end Ola settled in the forest for good, in her little cottage.
> And it seems that is exactly where she found her place.
> Because some elves do not go looking for their forest.
> **One day they simply return to it.**
