# The Cottage of the Two Brothers

> Two Elves, twins, share one cottage. One rises at dawn, the other — at dusk. They have seen each other only twice in their lives.

They leave each other little notes under a stone. Only the forest knows them. If one day one of those notes flutters your way on the wind — read it with a smile and let it fly on.

## Who lives here

**The Two Brothers**, who teach wanderers the virtue of _brotherhood_.

## What to do when you reach the cottage?

1. Pause for a moment, close your eyes and listen to the forest.
2. Find the plaque near the cottage.
3. Scan the QR code or enter the four-digit code on the main page to hear the Elf's voice.
4. Leave the cottage just as you found it — the forest remembers.
