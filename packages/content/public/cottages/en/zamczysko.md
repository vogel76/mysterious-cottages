# The Cottage on Zamczysko Hill

> At the top of Zamczysko Hill, where the rocks arrange themselves into a rampart, stands a cottage full of echoes of ancient battles.

Its resident has heard the stones humming old knightly songs. If you come quietly and lay your hand on a boulder — you will feel that the heart of the hill still beats.

## Who lives here

**The Guardian of Zamczysko Hill**, who teaches wanderers the virtue of _remembrance_.

## What to do when you reach the cottage?

1. Pause for a moment, close your eyes and listen to the forest.
2. Find the plaque near the cottage.
3. Scan the QR code or enter the four-digit code on the main page to hear the Elf's voice.
4. Leave the cottage just as you found it — the forest remembers.
