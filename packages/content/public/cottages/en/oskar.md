# Oskar, Marta and Teo

> Not so long ago two elves appeared in the area: Oskar and Marta, who until then had believed that the best address is "somewhere further on".
> They had lived in big cities, beyond the mountains, by the sea, and even in a country where everything is almost perfect.
> Some collect souvenirs. They collected places, scents and stories. Because some elves are born with roots, and others… with a map in their pocket.
> Oskar cooked in restaurants that collected stars. One day, though, he decided that stars cannot be spread with butter. A loaf can. And that is when he fulfilled his small dream: he opened a bakery.
> Not forever.
> For now.
> Because even the longest journey sometimes deserves a stop that smells of fresh bread.
> Then Teodor came along.
> And suddenly it turned out that you can dream of faraway expeditions and, at the same time, delight in someone taking their first steps between the sacks of flour.
> Marta became a mum, but she still has a gift that makes pain remember, very quickly, that it has something to do elsewhere.
> In the evenings the three of them sit in front of the cottage.
> Oskar spreads out the map.
> Marta points to places they have not visited yet.
> Teodor picks a completely random spot with his finger and says:
> — Here!
> And Oskar just smiles.
> Because he knows very well that the bakery was a dream.
> But not the last one.
> The moral:
> Some elves build a home. Others build a place that is always worth coming back to… before setting off again.
> This moral leaves the door open, exactly the way Oskar and Marta live. They have not given up on travelling. They have simply stopped for a while where it smells of bread.
