# The Cottage on the Rock Spires

> The cottage hangs between three white crags. The wind that climbs up there brings news from distant valleys.

Its resident knows the speech of the rocks — and rocks, as everyone knows, are reserved, but once they finally speak, you remember it forever.

## Who lives here

**The Elf of the Rock Spires**, who teaches wanderers the virtue of *perseverance*.

## What to do when you reach the cottage?

1. Pause for a moment, close your eyes and listen to the forest.
2. Find the plaque near the cottage.
3. Scan the QR code or enter the four-digit code on the main page to hear the Elf's voice.
4. Leave the cottage just as you found it — the forest remembers.
