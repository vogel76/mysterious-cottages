# The Lonely Elf's Cottage

> At the edge of a quiet forest, on a high rock — right here — settled an Elf named Fredek.
> 
> All his life he had been an artist, but one day he felt he had lost the sense of creating. So he withdrew from the others and settled in a small, minimalist house on the cliff.
> — But just look at that view… see for yourselves — he would sometimes say, gazing into the distance.
> Fredek often sat on the rock planning yet more small changes to his house, as if he never wanted to finish it.
> But what he loved most was painting… trees.
> If you have ever seen eyes on tree trunks — calm ones, or smiling — that was his work.
> Sometimes he also wrote short poems on leaves and bark, which vanished with the wind.
> And it was here, in the silence, that Fredek slowly remembered what he truly wanted to do.
> To create. In his own way. He says: "Loneliness doesn't hurt when you have the forest for a friend." If you sit down beside him and say nothing — it will be the best conversation you have ever had.

## Who lives here

**The Lonely Elf**, who teaches wanderers the virtue of *contemplation*.

## What to do when you reach the cottage?

1. Pause for a moment, close your eyes and listen to the forest.
2. Find the plaque near the cottage.
3. Scan the QR code or enter the four-digit code on the main page to hear the Elf's voice.
4. Leave the cottage just as you found it — the forest remembers.
