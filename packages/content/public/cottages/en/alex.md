# The Cottage of the Elves Jędrzej and Franek

> A pair of Elves, Jędrzej and Franek, the brothers of this very cottage, lived for a long time by the millpond at Kacper Brandys's mill.
> 
> They liked that place. The water ran fast there, something was always happening, and the sounds of the mill and the sawmill at work never went quiet.
> But one day everything fell silent.
> The wheels stopped turning, the boards stopped singing under the saw, and the water grew calm.
> The Elves sat in front of their little house for a long while, wondering what to do next.
> Until one of them said:
> — Maybe not everything that stops is an ending.
> They packed up their belongings and set off along the Będkówka river.
> Not far — just far enough to find a new place.
> A few hundred metres on, they reached the Szum waterfall.
> They sat down there only for a moment.
> But when they heard the water and felt the calm of that place, they decided to stay.
> They built a new cottage, and ever since they have kept watch over the river's current.
> They do not do much.
> They watch, they listen, and they remind everyone who finds them that not everything has to run like a mill!!!
> Sometimes it is good to simply sit down and listen to the murmur.

## Who lives here

**The brothers Jędrzej and Franek**, who tell wanderers: *take it easy*.
