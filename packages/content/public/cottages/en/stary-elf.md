# The Old Elf's Cottage

> The Old Elf remembers the first snowfall on this clearing. He has seen so many springs that he stopped counting long ago.

He has only one chair, yet he invites everyone in. He says the guest may sit and he may stand — because after all these years, he has done quite enough sitting.

## Who lives here

**The Old Elf**, who teaches wanderers the virtue of *wisdom*.

## What to do when you reach the cottage?

1. Pause for a moment, close your eyes and listen to the forest.
2. Find the plaque near the cottage.
3. Scan the QR code or enter the four-digit code on the main page to hear the Elf's voice.
4. Leave the cottage just as you found it — the forest remembers.
