# The Cottage of Gwerda and Filinek

> Gwerda is an Elf-maid, and Filinek — a small, fluffy owl who keeps watch on the roof of the cottage.

Together they make the best pair in all of Chatynkowo: Gwerda knows what happens by day, Filinek — what happens by night. Together they see the whole world.

## Who lives here

**Gwerda and Filinek**, who teach wanderers the virtue of *friendship*.

## What to do when you reach the cottage?

1. Pause for a moment, close your eyes and listen to the forest.
2. Find the plaque near the cottage.
3. Scan the QR code or enter the four-digit code on the main page to hear the Elf's voice.
4. Leave the cottage just as you found it — the forest remembers.
