# The Castle Guardian's Cottage

> The Guardian's cottage stands close to the ruins of a real castle. At night you can hear the clink of armour from there — that is the Guardian exercising his memory.

He guards not the walls, but the stories. He says that every castle that falls sinks into human hearts like a seed, and one day a story grows out of it.

## Who lives here

**The Castle Guardian**, who teaches wanderers the virtue of *loyalty*.

## What to do when you reach the cottage?

1. Pause for a moment, close your eyes and listen to the forest.
2. Find the plaque near the cottage.
3. Scan the QR code or enter the four-digit code on the main page to hear the Elf's voice.
4. Leave the cottage just as you found it — the forest remembers.
