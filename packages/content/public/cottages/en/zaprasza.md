# The Cottage That Invites

> This cottage looks for guests all by itself. Its door opens slightly whenever someone with a good heart passes nearby.

A very shy Elf lives in it, one who never speaks first. But if you sit down on the doorstep and quietly say "hello" — you will hear him answer in a warm whisper.

## Who lives here

**The Cottage That Invites**, which teaches wanderers the virtue of *invitation*.

## What to do when you reach the cottage?

1. Pause for a moment, close your eyes and listen to the forest.
2. Find the plaque near the cottage.
3. Scan the QR code or enter the four-digit code on the main page to hear the Elf's voice.
4. Leave the cottage just as you found it — the forest remembers.
