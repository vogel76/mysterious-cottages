# The Cottage of Tupak the Elf

> Tupak got his name because he likes to stomp on an oak trunk to wake the dragonflies in the pond near his cottage.

He claims the world falls asleep when nobody listens to it. That is why every morning he knocks on the tree three times — once for himself, once for the forest, and once for someone who will feel sad today.

## Who lives here

**Tupak the Elf**, who teaches wanderers the virtue of _mindfulness_.

## What to do when you reach the cottage?

1. Pause for a moment, close your eyes and listen to the forest.
2. Find the plaque near the cottage.
3. Scan the QR code or enter the four-digit code on the main page to hear the Elf's voice.
4. Leave the cottage just as you found it — the forest remembers.
