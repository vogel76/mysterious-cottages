# Wawrzyniec's Cottage

> Recently, out of nowhere, beavers appeared in the area — and they decided to settle right here.
> Wawrzyniec the Elf was simply delighted, because at last he has someone to swim with — and not only that :)

## Who lives here

**Wawrzyniec**, who teaches wanderers the virtue of *old age is a joy*.

## What to do when you reach the cottage?

1. Pause for a moment, close your eyes and listen to the forest.
2. Find the plaque near the cottage.
3. Scan the QR code or enter the four-digit code on the main page to hear the Elf's voice.
4. Leave the cottage just as you found it — the forest remembers.
