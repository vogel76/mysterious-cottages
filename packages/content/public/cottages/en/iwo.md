# The Cottage of Iwo the Elf

> Iwo lives the farthest away of all the Elves — where the forests meet the meadows, and the raspberry fields smell of summer long after sunset.

Whenever Iwo climbs his stone steps, he always carries a handful of nuts for wanderers. Whoever accepts the gift with a smile receives in return a whisper of the wind from the valley.

## Who lives here

**Iwo the Elf**, who teaches wanderers the virtue of *hospitality*.

## What to do when you reach the cottage?

1. Pause for a moment, close your eyes and listen to the forest.
2. Find the plaque near the cottage.
3. Scan the QR code or enter the four-digit code on the main page to hear the Elf's voice.
4. Leave the cottage just as you found it — the forest remembers.
