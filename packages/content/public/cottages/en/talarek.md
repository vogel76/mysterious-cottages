# Talarek

> Not so long ago an elf named Talarek moved into this cottage.
> He chose this spot because it seems to him as safe as the fortress standing next to it :)
> Talarek is tiny, grey-haired and plump, and his favourite phrase is "give me". At the same time, Talarek considers himself an exceptionally generous elf. As he sees it, he helps everyone, he gives to everyone, and nobody ever gives him anything.
> The trouble is that Talarek has an unusually complicated relationship with money.
> He likes it very much.
> Best of all when it stays exactly where it is.
> When Talarek has to buy something, a serious event begins. First he looks at the thing for a long time. Then he checks the price. Then he sighs, puts it back in its place and goes home, extremely pleased with a successful saving.
> Soon he becomes so thrifty that he starts saving even on his own comfort. He switches the light on only when he really has to see something, and he uses one tea bag for so long that it starts to look more tired than he is.
> One day, however, Talarek discovered that his biggest problem is not a lack of money.
> His problem is that every time he could buy something, do something or give someone a gift, he first checks how much he will lose on it.
> And suddenly it turns out that all his life he has guarded his coins so diligently that he forgets to enjoy what they could give him.
> The moral:
> Stinginess begins when you are more afraid of spending money than sorry that you did not spend it on something that was truly worth its price.
