Not long ago…

…there lived an elf named Boguś. Just an ordinary Boguś. True, sometimes a Bogumił gets born instead, but that's not the case here.

With his own two hands, Boguś built himself a cottage of clay and stone right in the middle of Łabajowa Cave. Any sensible elf would say it's damp, dark and it stinks — but Boguś decided that at least the neighbours don't drill. ;)

Boguś is never still. There isn't a moment when he's doing nothing. Well… except when he's asleep. Then even the bats tiptoe.

Every day he bustles around the cave cleaning up after people. Because people have a remarkable gift. They can stand in awe of a million-year-old rock, take fifty photos with it, and then leave behind a mess as if they meant to contribute a geological layer of their own.

Boguś mutters something under his breath, calls them a few names, then sweeps up and goes back to his own business. And he has plenty of it. He even helped out on the shoot for that music video by Dawid — the one who "sits down." Nobody there noticed him, but they say the stones for the campfire arranged themselves. Coincidence? Elves don't believe in coincidences.

Do you know why Boguś settled in this particular spot?

Because elves know something that people tend to remember only when it's already too late!

Boguś maintains that as long as you have a reason to get out of bed in the morning, you can safely stuff your birth certificate in a drawer. And even the elves don't argue with that.

Moral? The ones who are needed most are often the very ones somebody wrote off too soon.
